COLONY V System Architecture

COLONY V is an autonomous video production agent that converts breaking RSS news into high-impact vertical short videos (1080x1920) and publishes them directly to YouTube.

1. Monitor & Research

Scans RSS feeds, scores news priority, and extracts verified claims with self-healing scrapers.

2. Script & Produce

Generates hook/body/CTA narration scripts, TTS voiceovers, SFX, and Remotion renders.

3. Publish & Learn

Publishes to YouTube Data API and analyzes channel metrics to tune future runs.
